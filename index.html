What the controls mean

Natural mode
Uses speaker-adaptive pitch analysis, conservative parameter limits, time-local spectral shaping, and optional automatic backoff when artifact checks fail.
Pitch median
Moves the overall F0 contour up or down while keeping the utterance duration approximately fixed.
Resonance
Uses Praat formant scaling. This is independent of the requested pitch shift.
Intonation
Expands or compresses variation around the utterance's pitch median.
Brightness
An experimental spectral-tilt control. Treat it as exploratory, not as a clinical target.
